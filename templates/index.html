The Pāṇinian Framework: Sthāna & Prayatna

Canonical articulatory science from the Pāṇinīya Śikṣā (पाणिनीय शिक्षा).

स्थान (Sthāna - Place of Articulation)

Every sound in Sanskrit originates from one of 5 distinct anatomical vocal regions: Kaṇṭha (Throat), Tālu (Hard Palate), Mūrdhā (Roof Dome), Danta (Teeth), and Oṣṭha (Lips).

Panini Sūtra:
ऌतुलसानां दन्ताः | ऋटुरषाणां मूर्धा

प्रयत्न (Prayatna - Articulatory Effort)

Controls acoustic breath and vocal cord vibration. Alpaprāṇa (gentle breath) vs Mahāprāṇa (aspiration), and Ghoṣa (voiced vibration) vs Aghoṣa (unvoiced pure articulation).

Varga Division:
१ व ३ = अल्पप्राण | २ व ४ = महाप्राण
